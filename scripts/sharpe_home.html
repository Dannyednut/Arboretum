Sharpe Terminal is an AI-driven trading intelligence terminal for crypto traders. It turns real-time data from 50+ exchanges into decision-ready signals across derivatives, DEX markets, on-chain risk, narratives, ecosystems, sentiment, and agent-ready workflows. It covers perpetual funding rates across 33 exchanges, futures open interest and liquidations, DEX token screening across 68 chains, smart contract rug-pull detection, cross-exchange arbitrage scanning, and sector rotation across 30 narrative categories and 23 blockchain ecosystems. The platform is built by Sharpe AI (founded 2021) and backed by Google Cloud, NVIDIA, AWS, and Anthropic.

Sharpe Terminal and Coinglass both cover crypto derivatives, but differ in scope and workflow. Coinglass focuses on derivatives aggregation (funding rates, open interest, liquidations) with most advanced features behind a paid subscription. Sharpe covers the same derivatives surface and adds DEX screening, narrative and ecosystem intelligence, correlation matrices, rug-pull detection, memecoin intelligence, rules-based directional scoring with heuristic scenarios, and an agent-ready API layer. Sharpe is best for traders who want derivatives signals in context with on-chain flow, narrative rotation, and AI-driven research; Coinglass is best for traders who only need derivatives data and prefer its specific UI.

A perpetual funding rate is a periodic payment exchanged between long and short traders in a perpetual futures contract, designed to keep the perpetual price anchored to the spot price. When funding is positive, longs pay shorts, which indicates bullish positioning and leveraged long pressure. When funding is negative, shorts pay longs, which indicates bearish positioning or a short squeeze setup. Funding rates are typically paid every 8 hours on Binance, Bybit, and OKX, and every hour on Hyperliquid and dYdX. Annualized funding rates (APR) above +50% often signal overcrowded longs and precede liquidation cascades; deeply negative funding can signal capitulation bottoms. Sharpe Terminal tracks funding rates across 33 exchanges in real time.
